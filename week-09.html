Week 9 · October 25, 2026

Cut It on Purpose

Key Learning Moments

This week we learned how to actually pitch. The professor went through problem, solution, impact, and the ask, and the thing I kept thinking about was how much of a pitch is about the person and not the product. You start with someone real going through the problem, not "users." You show one person using what you built instead of listing every feature. And you have to say why AI is really needed, not just that you used it. He also said the ending should go back to the person you started with, and that "so yeah, that's our project" is the worst way to finish. One thing he said that I remember is that a great idea explained badly loses to a good idea explained well.

After that we talked about scope, which honestly I did not expect to care about as much as I did. He said everything takes about three times longer than you think, and that last year most of the build time went into fixing bugs instead of adding new things. He also said every student who did well last year had to cut features. Then he asked us what the smallest version of our idea is that still proves it works, and said if your must-have list is more than five things, you have not cut enough.

Personal Connections

While he was going through the pitch part, I kept thinking about my Week 8 video. I opened it with one person and one line, "it's just a small cold," which is pretty much what he was describing. But I never came back to that man at the end. I ended on what I still had to test. Next time I want the ending to go back to where I started.

Challenges and Growth

The scope part was the one that hit me. I looked at my own prototype and it already has a lot going on: the daily call, the summary, the family app, the trust settings, the doctor alert. That is way more than five. So I asked myself his question, and the honest answer is one daily call to one parent and a short note to the family. That's it. The tricky part is that noticing when someone sounds different is what makes my idea special, and it is also the hardest thing to build. So I will probably start simple and make it smarter later.
