Week 7 · October 11, 2026

From Prototype to Product

Key Learning Moments

This week was a lighter one. We looked at more tools, Lovable and Claude in particular, and how to code better with them rather than just getting something to run. Then we came back to customer discovery and outreach, which has been the thread through the whole course.

The new part was the discussion of our prototypes from last week. Not a review of each one, more a conversation about what happens next: how a low-fidelity prototype turns into an MVP, what from the mockup is actually usable in real life and what is not, and which coding tools fit which part of that build.

Personal Connections

The coding-tools part was familiar ground. What was less familiar was looking at my own prototype through the "can this actually be used" lens. My core feature is a daily phone call to a parent in India and an honest report to the son abroad. On a screen it is clean. In real use the hard parts are not on the screen: the call has to work on a keypad phone, the voice has to be worth answering, and the report has to be right often enough that the family keeps trusting it. The MVP is those three things, not the app.

Challenges and Growth

Outreach was the uncomfortable part again. I have done three real interviews now, and I still have not talked to a single older person, the one who would actually pick up the phone. Every week I get a little further outside my circle and stop. The growth is that I know exactly which conversation I am avoiding.

AI Tool Documentation

I talked this reflection out with voice to text and used Claude to organize it into sections. The transcription turned the course name into "AI 25" again and merged several sentences into one run-on, so Claude had to ask what I meant in a couple of places instead of guessing. Same lesson every week: read the draft against what I actually said.
