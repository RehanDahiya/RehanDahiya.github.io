Week 6 · October 4, 2026

Gray Boxes Before Polish

Key Learning Moments

This week was all prototyping. Why you do it before investing in development: to communicate the idea clearly to the team and stakeholders, to iterate quickly, and to decide whether you are going for functionality or visual polish. We went through the fidelity levels, sketches, paper prototypes, digital mockups, and the AI-enhanced version of each, like wireframes generated from a text description.

We also covered which tool fits which kind of prototyping, Canva AI, Gemini image generation, Adobe Firefly, AI wireframing tools, and the thread through all of it was that the prototype has to reflect what people told you, not what you assumed.

The slide I will keep was the one on doing low fidelity well. Focus on the core functionality. Use realistic placeholder content, not lorem ipsum. Grayscale and simple shapes are enough. Then the storyboard pitfalls: starting with your solution instead of the user, making the journey too linear, ignoring emotions, skipping failure scenarios. And the best practices: root every step in customer interviews, include realistic friction and contradiction like real humans have, show emotional progression, test with real users.

Personal Connections

I built my prototype and storyboard this week straight from the Week 5 interviews, so the pitfalls list read like a checklist of things I could have gotten wrong. The one that landed was skipping failure scenarios. My first storyboard had one failure in eight stages, and everything else was smooth. Real families are not smooth. My mother spent her whole interview on what happens if the AI breaks or manipulates her mother, and that deserved its own stage.

Challenges and Growth

I ignored the grayscale advice. My mockups have color, and I can see why the professor warns against it: a colored screen looks finished, so people react to the look instead of the flow. Next pass I would strip it back to boxes. The other gap is testing. Nobody outside my family has touched the prototype yet. Same gap as every week, one level up.

AI Tool Documentation
